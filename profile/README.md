## About

**LATIS Medical Imaging** is an open research and engineering initiative focused on medical image processing, artificial intelligence, and medical imaging infrastructure.

Our goal is to bridge the gap between **medical imaging research** and **reusable, scalable software**.

---

## 🧬 Medical Image Standard

Our main framework, **`medical-image-std`**, provides a modular foundation for medical image processing and AI applications.

Key features include:

* DICOM and medical image processing
* Image filtering and enhancement
* Thresholding and morphological operations
* Patch-based processing
* GPU acceleration with PyTorch
* Medical image annotations
* Dataset and COCO integration
* Extensible AI and image-processing pipelines
